# Rubric calibration v4

`rubrics-v4.json` is a reviewed overlay on the **unchanged** `gold.json`, not a replacement gold answer key. This assessment uses only the repository's canonical corpus and the saved audit in `eval/AUDIT.md`; it made no answerer or judge calls. Every one of the 50 gold question IDs has `reviewStatus: "reviewed"`, its exact original question and SHA-256 digest, an ordered copy of every exact original criterion, a dimension, actual canonical source chunk IDs and text hashes, and a short source-reviewed rationale. There are no licensed source passages in the overlay.

## Integration contract

- Reject a changed `gold.json` byte hash, missing/extra question IDs, changed question-text hash, reordered or altered original criteria, and mismatched source text hashes. `baselineSha256` hashes the whole file; `originalQuestionSha256` hashes the exact UTF-8 question string. Each `sources[].sha256` hashes `chunkText(workspace, row)` from `src/tp/manifest.ts`, not its Markdown/frontmatter or the comparison export's ID wrapper; `hashOf` specifies this explicitly.
- Map each ordered criterion to `replacement ?? original` for a **separately named** v4 judge experiment. A null replacement means unchanged, including its historical source-family condition. Keep each original, dimension, rationale, and ambiguity flag in the grading provenance. Never mutate gold or historical scores. A replacement is a review instruction, not a model answer to give the answerer.
- `ambiguity.needsAdjudication: true` means the item cannot enter an **unambiguous legal-accuracy denominator** until a legal reviewer resolves the stated gap. It is still retained for diagnostics and accounting of all 50 planned questions; grading it does not convert its score into an adjudicated correctness claim. The ten flagged IDs are q01, q02, q08, q12, q14, q34, q36, q41, q44 and q45. Do not silently supply a company form, jurisdiction, transaction subtype, or new paired-comparison question.
- Keep legal correctness, completeness, required-source-family compliance, and grounding separate. A correct claim with an equivalent statutory source outside a frozen required family is not a legal error; an answer citing a chunk the model never actually saw is not thereby grounded. Source hashes here support rubric review, **not** proof of model-visible evidence. Preserve strict historical family scores as their own measurement, and preserve citation syntax validity independently. An inapplicable criterion such as q31's unasked comparison must not be turned into a factual failure or quietly dropped from the frozen denominator; disclose any changed v4 denominator or neutral treatment.
- Replay saved answers and only the sources truly visible to each original answerer. Do not inject this overlay's reviewed sources into the evidence inventory, repair answers, or describe a replay as a retrieval/agent improvement. Compare policies on the same answer hashes, judge configuration and evidence protocol; report operational failures and ambiguous cases apart from legal rate. The first 15 are the overlapping development comparison cap, not an independent holdout or a 99% claim.
