# Hypothetical sponsor mandate — Cedar Capital

These are invented client facts, not facts in the legal corpus. Assume Luxembourg law as compiled through 29 May 2026 and a Luxembourg holding vehicle to be formed in 2026. Cedar Capital's institutional investor requires its holding vehicle to have legal personality so it can itself own a property portfolio. The team wants contractual distribution flexibility, interests rather than publicly marketed shares, and no present need for an equity-security issue. Cedar can supply a separately incorporated Luxembourg corporate general partner; the investor will be a limited partner. Neither party wants the investor to incur general-partner liability.

The investor wants a veto over major acquisitions and the right to advise the manager, and its deal lead proposes to sign acquisition contracts simply as "Cedar investor" without a representative capacity. The sponsor's draft pitch describes the SCSp as a separate legal person and treats all limited-partner activity as prohibited management. The committee requests a form recommendation, a rejected-alternative matrix, and a short list of closing diligence items; do not opine on unprovided tax or regulatory facts.
