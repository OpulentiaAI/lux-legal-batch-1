# Intent

Maintain evidence-grounded knowledge of the legal harness's engineering contracts and separately named evaluations, preserving source identities, recorded configurations, historical attempt states, evidence availability and review decisions. The graph should let an operator find the governing protocol and its original source, tell a retained checkpoint from accepted execution or independent grading, and distinguish policy changes from measured outcomes. Capture metadata without granting access to original evaluator-only material; admit interpretations only through source-grounded extraction, cut and resolution.

# Out-of-scope

- Legal answers, licensed source bodies, task deliverables, grading rubrics, gold answers and secret or authenticated provider material.
- Canonical claims inferred solely from a registered tool, mocked handler, stored configuration or unreviewed candidate.
- Legal scores or successful execution synthesized from failed, interrupted, planned or historically running checkpoints.
- Full consumption totals inferred from known subtotals, overlapping accounting copies or absent data.
- Fresh provider connectivity or cleanup claims inferred from stored attestations.
- Performance improvements inferred from unpaired runs, overlapping intervals or a changed model, budget, protocol or grading policy.
- Direct score comparisons that replace the named closed-corpus baseline with broader-access experiments.
- Blueprint, voice, wiki, repository-Q&A, user-management and settings capabilities without an explicit scope change.
