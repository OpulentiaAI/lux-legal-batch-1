# Hypothetical client intake — Atlas SA

These are invented instructions, not facts asserted by the legal corpus. Assume the law of Luxembourg as compiled through 29 May 2026 applies and the contemplated vote is 21 December 2026.

- Atlas SA is a Luxembourg société anonyme with its registered office and splitting-company register in Luxembourg. It plans a cross-border **complete** split into two newly incorporated French SAS beneficiaries, Rhône SAS and Loire SAS, whose registered offices and incorporation law will be French. Assume both French SAS are eligible Annex II companies, subject to confirmation by local counsel. All assets and liabilities will be allocated between them, Atlas will cease to exist, and Atlas shareholders will receive beneficiary shares. There is no cash adjustment.
- Atlas is not in liquidation or regulated resolution; it has employees in Luxembourg. No shareholder has waived any report or expert review. A French lawyer has not yet certified the beneficiary incorporation, registration, or worker-participation arrangements. Do not assert those steps complete.
- The board wants to announce that 21 December is the **legal effective date** merely because shareholders approved the split that day. The project manager instead needs a legally defensible sequence and a date-dependency note for financing drawdown and contract migration.
