# Hypothetical closing instructions

Orion SA in Luxembourg plans to retain its business and transfer an identified division to two **existing Luxembourg companies**, Vega SA and Lyra SARL. Orion will not dissolve. The project memorandum proposes allocation, but no signed split instrument, concordant company decisions, publication proof, creditor records, or completed closing certificate is attached. Lyra also proposes an articles amendment to admit a new financing arrangement, with no articles or shareholder vote record attached. Separately, Orion expects a dividend from a third-party investment, but no distributing-company general-meeting decision is attached. The client asks whether accounting should mark all three workstreams completed this week. No creditor has agreed to a release in the materials.
