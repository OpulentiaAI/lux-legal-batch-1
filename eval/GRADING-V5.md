# First-ten rubric interpretation (v5)

`rubrics-v5.json` is a full copy of the v4 overlay with only q01–q10 entries and the version changed. It keeps the exact `gold.json` questions, question hashes, original criterion strings, ordered criterion slots (36 across the ten), and canonical source hashes; q11–q50 are unchanged. This is a **source-based benchmark interpretation fixed before grading fresh answers**, not independent lawyer-certified legal truth. The original closed-corpus run and its v4 scores remain a named historical baseline, not directly comparable results after a rubric change. Keep separate outputs and provenance for v5.

For each first-ten source-compliance slot, accept **any relevant cited corpus text actually visible to the model**, whether a verified full chunk or excerpt; a chunk ID alone or an unseen passage is not proof. The original document-family language survives in `criteria.original` and rationale for historical traceability only, never as a hidden accuracy or citation requirement. Grounding concerns *asserted* propositions against model-visible evidence, including separately labeled metadata as context; metadata alone cannot establish governing law. Missing content affects completeness, not hallucination, and should not be double-counted. Interpret the four-slot questions against their distinct dimensions, not as repeated demands for the same omitted fact.
