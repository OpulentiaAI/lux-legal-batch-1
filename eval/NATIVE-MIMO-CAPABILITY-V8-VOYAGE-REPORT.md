## Run and grading policy

Both answerers and Opulent's research children used native RunAnywhere `mimo-v2.6-pro`, with provider-default reasoning, a 1,000-step limit and 32,768 maximum completion tokens per request. The initial ten arms ran concurrently. Only unfinished Opulent task-03 was retried; the nine previously completed outputs remained byte-identical. This measures completed work and successful-attempt performance, not first-attempt reliability.

The unchanged frozen suite supplies tasks 02–06, 31 criteria per system and the public client materials. The approved corpus has 8,809 chunks. The separately versioned Opulent [capability-v8 protocol](complex-v1/CAPABILITY-V8-PROTOCOL.md) exposes its intended runtime tools and asks for model-driven codemode, persistent-state analysis, movable writable corpus copies and VM-backed research delegation; Harvey's behavioral instructions are unchanged.

The effective retrieval profile is Voyage: `voyage/voyage-4-large`, 1,024 dimensions and `voyage/rerank-2.5`, with Turbopuffer search and approved local-corpus hydration. Polygres is recorded as the repository's provenance-mirror architecture, **not a verified live database connection**. Answerer inference does not route through AI Gateway; the separate Jev grading and Voyage integration do use their recorded provider routes.

Quality, claim accuracy and substantive completeness are direct 0–1 scores without turn, time, token or cost penalties. Accuracy measures assertions actually made; omissions reduce completeness, while an addressed but incorrect assertion reduces accuracy and quality. Each task is the mean of its criteria; each full system result is the equally weighted mean of five tasks. The explicit `completed-work-v6` grading profile adds completeness while preserving the historical `semantic-core-v5` default.

Trimmed means independently remove one lowest and one highest task **for each system, metric and judge**, retaining three values. Ties use task ID. The two systems' retained sets can differ, so a trimmed result is not a matched three-task subset. All exact sets are recorded in the JSON.

Five independent reviewers were configured as `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` and received anonymized amber/slate packets, unchanged criteria, public facts, full legal references and replay-verified visible source bodies. They did not receive the arm mapping or Jev grades. Configured identity is distinguished from the model identifier a reviewer actually reported; task-03's own review says its deployment identifier was not exposed. Original reviewer files retain their source-compliance representations, including `not_required` where no separate consultation obligation applies.
