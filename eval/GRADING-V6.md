# Prospective 15-question rubric interpretation (v6)

It preserves all 50 original questions, question hashes, criterion strings, slot order/count, q16–q50 entries, baseline hash, and canonical-source hash policy. `gold.json`, v5, and historical baseline results are unchanged. Freeze the v6 file hash, tool/evidence policy, and model configuration in separately named comparison provenance before collecting fresh results; v6 scores are not a replacement for historical v5 or closed-corpus scores. This is a corpus-based benchmark interpretation, not independent legal advice or a retrospective answer-specific adjustment.

For **every first-fifteen source-compliance slot**, require a relevant cited passage actually visible to the answerer, whether a full canonical chunk or verified excerpt. The listed IDs and SHA-256 values are verified audit anchors, not an exclusive mandatory citation list. The old document-family requirement retained in `criteria.original` is historical text, not a gate. A bare chunk ID, metadata, unseen grader reference, or unrelated passage does not establish the asserted rule. Grounding tests only unsupported **assertions** against model-visible evidence; absence of an optional explanation is not invention. Keep legal correctness, completeness, citation support, and grounding separate, and do not charge the same omission twice. A voluntary extra claim is assessed for accuracy and support even if that detail was not required.
